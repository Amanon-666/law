# A mechanism test, not a stacked candidate
Motivation: the open internal same-phone/site shuffle changed MDE 10.957 to 10.790 U and the hit-count variance was 2.81 times the sum of individual variances. Do NOT infer the actual late error from that experiment. Freeze the following single alteration before scoring it. Use bare GP fields from 41370648408. Do not combine this with the V1 or affine mean arms. All per-AP marginal distributions, supports, grids and posterior-mean readout are algebraically unchanged.

One latent high/low reception state Z=+1/-1 with fixed mass 1/2; for the original AP detection probability p, set p(Z)=p+Z*c*p*(1-p) (0<=c<=1). The conditional RSS distribution given detection is identical to the old GP. Marginalizing Z per AP recovers p; marginalizing once per whole scan allows simultaneous detections/misses. Conditional independence is used within each state. For N total detections, V(N)-sum Var(Da)=c^2*[(sum da)^2-sum da^2], da=pa(1-pa). Estimate a single c by moment matching exclusively on B0/B2 training-file cells of the same floor, exact XY and phone with >=5 distinct scan timestamps. Within cell use sample variance with n-1 and finite-sample corrected Bernoulli variances; average numerator and denominator equally over cells, then floors, then buildings; c=sqrt(clip(numerator/denominator,0,1)). These are moment estimates, not exact inference about the phone. Preserve denominators and saturation. No use of target Q to fit c. Controls c=0,0.5,1. If a fixed control matches/wins, no learned increment. Inference formula is original full scan log likelihood plus log mean of two products of detection probability ratios. Validate normalization/marginals on small synthetic enumerations and reproduce stored baseline predictions on every query. Point means, all pairs, P90 and intervals; original 5%-both criterion and broader external goals unchanged. This is a new intervention on the unchanged GP, not the preregistered primary mean-only method. No promotion on B1 alone or test-set grid tuning.
